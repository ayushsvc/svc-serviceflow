======================================================================
1. SCOPE AND IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
======================================================================

This research compares the current SVC ServiceFlow implementation with
publicly available HaloITSM product and capability information.

HaloITSM public materials describe product capabilities, but they do not
provide complete tenant-level technical specifications. The following
details require private Halo tenant documentation, configuration exports,
or vendor confirmation:

- Exact role and permission matrices
- Sub-administrator scope boundaries
- Field-level security
- Workflow state and transition schemas
- Approval and escalation semantics
- SLA calendar, pause, and breach formulas
- Service catalog data model and request variables
- CMDB schema and relationship types
- Automation API contracts, retries, and rate limits
- Reporting query model and row-level security
- Backup, restore, data residency, and release procedures
- Differences between HaloITSM, HaloPSA, HaloCRM, and newer Halo products

This document is an alignment reference, not a claim that SVC ServiceFlow
is affiliated with or identical to HaloITSM. SVC ServiceFlow should follow
comparable ITIL-oriented practices using original implementation, code,
branding, and assets.

3. HALOITSM PUBLIC CAPABILITY INVENTORY
======================================================================

3.1 Core ITSM and ITIL processes

Public Halo materials describe an all-inclusive, ITIL-aligned platform
covering:

- Incident management
- Service requests
- Change management
- Problem management
- Knowledge management
- Service-level management
- Asset management
- Configuration management database (CMDB)
- Automation
- Reporting and analytics

The public material establishes supported process areas, but does not
prove that every tenant is configured with identical ITIL states or
approval paths.

- A full catalog item and fulfillment model is not yet implemented.

Needed:

- Service catalog items
- Custom request fields
- Catalog-specific forms

2. Change management
3. Service catalog and request fulfillment
4. Knowledge management
5. Asset and CMDB foundation
6. Business calendars and mature SLA/OLA behavior
7. Major-incident management

======================================================================
8. FINAL CONCLUSION
======================================================================

SVC ServiceFlow is currently a strong ticketing and automation foundation,
but it is not yet HaloITSM-equivalent.

The largest capability gaps are:

1. Service catalog and request fulfillment
2. Problem management
3. Change management
4. Asset and CMDB management
5. Knowledge management
6. Mature SLA and OLA calendars
7. Major-incident management
8. Configurable reporting
9. Delegated administration and fine-grained permissions
10. Integrations and email collection

The product should become HaloITSM-aligned by implementing comparable
ITIL processes and operational controls while retaining:

- SVC ServiceFlow product identity
- SVC Infosystems branding
- Original source code
- Original UI implementation
- Original assets
- Independent data model and architecture

Do not copy HaloITSM source code, proprietary assets, text, interface
designs, or implementation details.
